# Verba

*Verba volant, scripta manent.* Local, offline transcription and OCR for macOS.

Verba turns audio and images into text **on your Mac**, without sending anything anywhere:

- **Voice notes and audio files → text.** Drop a Telegram or WhatsApp voice note (`.ogg`/Opus) or any audio
  file: the text lands in the clipboard and in a `.txt` next to the file.
- **Screenshots, images and PDFs → text or Markdown**, tables included.

Free, open source, native, with few settings. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

> **Status: not usable yet.** The project is at its first milestone. See [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).

## Why local

Transcribing a voice note or reading the text in a screenshot does not need a frontier model in a data
center: small models running on Apple Silicon do it well, for free, and without your recordings and
documents leaving your machine. Verba keeps those jobs local; the reasoning stays with whatever LLM you
prefer.

## Requirements

A Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 27 or later.
